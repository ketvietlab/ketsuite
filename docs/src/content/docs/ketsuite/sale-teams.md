---
title: Sales teams
description: Company sales teams, an order's salesperson and team, their defaults and search.
---

`sale.Team` is a company-scoped team: a name (at most 60 characters, unique in the company without regard to case, archived teams included; index `(companyId, nameKey)`), a leader, a monthly invoicing target in whole units of the company currency, an active flag and a revision id. `sale.TeamMember` names the salespeople in a team. Its `heldBy` column is the member while the team is in use and null once it is archived; the unique index `(companyId, heldBy)` lets a salesperson sell in at most one team in use, also when two saves race. Leaders and members are active users of the company (its memberships).

`sale.saveTeam` creates or edits an active team at its current revision id; `sale.setTeamActive` archives one, which frees its members, or restores one, which is refused while a member sells in another team in use. Both need bundle `sale.teams` (configure, configuration audit), which the sales manager and company administrator templates grant. `sale.listTeams` returns the teams by name with the leader's and members' names; anyone who reads Sales may list them. `sale.getTeamChoices` lists the company's active users as candidates.

`sale.Order` gains `userId` (salesperson) and `teamId`. `sale.createOrder` (and every writer that composes it: saving a draft, quotation templates, copies, CRM and follow-up quotations) takes the salesperson the caller names, else the customer's salesperson from `sale.CustomerTerms`, else the acting user when they belong to the company; the team is the one named, else the salesperson's team in use. An explicit `null` stays null; the storefront passes null for both. A copy keeps its salesperson while they still belong to the company. `sale.setOrderSalesperson` (bundle `sale.quote-operate`, idempotent) changes them on an order in any state without touching its revision: a new salesperson brings their team unless a team is named; an order keeps an archived team or a salesperson who left, and no other order takes them on. `sale.getOrderSales` reads both names. Writers that create orders read `ORDER_SALES_EFFECTS` (`user.Membership`, `user.User`, `sale.Team`, `sale.TeamMember`).

The configuration screen lives behind the rail's gear at `/admin/sales/teams`: an island list (search team or leader names without accents, status facets, sort by name or target) whose rows open the `sale.team` record modal with relation pickers for the leader and the members. The quotation and order page shows **Salesperson** and **Sales team** in its information card, offers both pickers when creating, and **Edit salesperson** opens a dialog with them. The sales order list matches team names in SQL, the quotation list in memory; `sale.listOrders` returns `userName` and `teamName`. Team overviews against targets belong to the sales analysis (D18, D19). VI/EN screen Help covers the screen and the order page.
